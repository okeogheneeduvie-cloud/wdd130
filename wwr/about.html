Splash Rapids Rafting

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide thrilling, safe, and memorable white water rafting experiences while promoting river conservation and a deep appreciation for the great outdoors. We believe every rapid tells a story and every journey brings people closer together.

History

Founded in 2010 by a group of passionate river guides, Splash Rapids Rafting started with a single raft and a big dream. Over the years, we have grown into a trusted rafting outfitter serving thousands of adventure seekers.

Today, we continue to provide exciting experiences for families, friends, schools, and groups while maintaining our commitment to safety, teamwork, and environmental stewardship.
